Subscription Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

QuantumCore WorkStation ("we", "us") publishes DueFlow : Subscription Manager (package com.dueflow.app). This policy explains how the app handles information. DueFlow is primarily an on-device expiry and renewal tracker; it does not require a DueFlow account.

Information you enter

You may record subscription names, amounts, categories, renewal and expiry dates, reminders, notes, document references and images or receipts you choose to import. This information is stored on your device in the app's local storage. We do not automatically upload your saved entries to a DueFlow cloud account, and there is no Google account or Google Drive synchronization in this release.

Files, camera and text recognition

If you choose to attach a file or scan text from a document or receipt, the selected content is used for your requested app function. Text recognition runs on-device using Google ML Kit. Google's SDK may process limited operational or diagnostic information under its own terms. Manually exported files, calendar exports and optional encrypted backups are stored or shared only where you choose; they are not automatically transmitted to us.

Notifications and device access

With your permission, DueFlow schedules local reminders and can restore their schedule after device restart. The app requests notification access and, where available, exact-alarm scheduling to deliver time-sensitive alerts. An optional app lock uses Android biometric authentication or a locally stored PIN verifier. DueFlow does not request your precise location, contacts, microphone recordings or advertising identifier.

Google Play services and purchases

DueFlow includes Google Play Billing and Play Integrity for optional paid features, purchase validation and abuse prevention. These Google services may collect and process transaction, app and device security information under Google's own policies. If paid purchase verification is enabled, relevant purchase tokens, a device-specific installation public key and Play Integrity attestation may be transmitted over HTTPS to our verification service to confirm entitlement. We do not receive your full payment-card number. We do not use third-party ad networks or sell your personal information.

Data sharing and retention

Your manually entered tracker records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Local export and backup files remain wherever you saved them; you control deletion of those copies. Payment and platform security records held by Google are governed by Google's retention policies. Where verification services are enabled, technical records are kept only for purchase verification, security, troubleshooting and legal compliance.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete individual items in the app.
  • Disable notifications or reminders through the app or Android settings.
  • Export or remove your local backups and files.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its local app-managed storage (subject to Android's backup and file-retention behavior).

Children

DueFlow is designed for general productivity and subscription management, not for children.

Changes

We may update this page when the app or applicable laws change. The date at the top indicates the latest version.
